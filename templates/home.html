Frequently Asked Questions

How long does sea freight take from China to Kenya?

Sea freight from major Chinese ports to Mombasa typically takes 25–35 days, depending on the shipping line and whether the vessel makes direct or transshipment stops.

Do you handle customs clearance?

Yes — we prepare and file all KRA documentation on your behalf, so your cargo clears customs without you needing to visit the port or border yourself.

Can I track my shipment online?

Yes, use your tracking number on our Track Shipment page for live status updates from pickup to delivery.

What documents do I need to ship cargo?

At minimum, a commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading or airway bill. We'll tell you if your specific cargo type needs anything additional.
